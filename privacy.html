Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

This policy covers LinkSet for iOS and macOS.

Bookmarks and iCloud

LinkSet stores bookmark URLs, titles, descriptions, groups, favorites, icons, and opening history on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, supported bookmark data is stored in your private CloudKit database associated with your Apple account and synchronizes between your devices. Regenerable webpage previews and automatic recovery snapshots are stored locally. Exported files may contain your bookmark data; you choose where to save or share them.

Webpage requests

To obtain titles, icons, and previews, or to check link availability, LinkSet may request the saved webpage and its resources. The destination website and its service providers receive normal network request information, such as your IP address and the requested URL. Their privacy practices apply to those requests.

Optional AI organization

AI organization is optional. After you enable AI and start an analysis, LinkSet sends the selected links’ URLs, titles, descriptions, current group information, and existing group names to the provider you select: DeepSeek, OpenAI, Google Gemini, or your configured compatible endpoint. Screenshots are not included in AI requests. Saving a bookmark does not automatically start AI analysis. API connection tests send a sample request rather than your bookmark library. Responses provide suggestions; moving links requires your confirmation.

AI credentials and choices

Your API keys are stored in this device’s system Keychain and used to authenticate requests to the selected endpoint. LinkSet does not store them in iCloud bookmark records. You can disable AI to stop new organization requests, remove a saved key in AI settings, or revoke the key with its provider. Disabling AI cannot retract data already sent. Each provider controls its own processing, retention, and deletion; review its policy before sending sensitive information, especially when using a custom endpoint.

Purchases

Apple processes App Store payments. LinkSet uses RevenueCat to load products, verify Pro access, and restore purchases. This involves purchase and transaction information and an anonymous app user identifier; LinkSet does not require a separate account or provide RevenueCat with your bookmark library or AI keys. LinkSet does not receive your payment card information. Apple and RevenueCat handle their service data under their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Bookmarks remain until you delete them. When sync is enabled, deletions can synchronize to your other devices. Turning off sync does not by itself erase previously synchronized iCloud data. Local automatic snapshots retain up to seven recovery files; deleting a bookmark does not immediately remove it from an existing snapshot or an exported backup. Preview caches can be cleared in settings. Remove exported files separately from wherever you saved them. Keychain credentials may survive uninstalling the app, so remove or revoke API keys separately. Uninstalling does not necessarily erase iCloud data, exported files, purchase records, or data already received by AI providers. Contact the responsible service for its deletion options; contact us for assistance with LinkSet data questions.

Device permissions and privacy groups

Biometric or device authentication uses Apple’s system authentication services; LinkSet does not obtain biometric templates. Clipboard contents are read when you explicitly choose to paste. Hidden and locked groups control visibility within LinkSet; they do not encrypt exported backups or replace device security. Protect your device and any files you export.

Contact and policy updates

LinkSet contains no advertising. For privacy questions or deletion assistance, contact linksetapp@gmail.com. We update this policy when data practices change and show the effective date with the updated policy.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 20 日

本政策适用于 iOS 和 macOS 版 LinkSet。

书签与 iCloud

LinkSet 在设备上保存书签网址、标题、描述、分组、收藏、图标和打开记录。开启 iCloud 同步后，支持同步的书签数据会保存到与你的 Apple 账户关联的私有 CloudKit 数据库，并在你的设备之间同步。可重新生成的网页预览和自动恢复快照保存在本机。导出文件可能包含书签数据，其保存和分享位置由你选择。

网页请求

为获取标题、图标、预览或检查链接是否有效，LinkSet 可能请求已保存的网页及其资源。目标网站及其服务提供商会收到正常网络请求信息，例如 IP 地址和请求的网址。这些请求适用相关网站的隐私规则。

可选的 AI 整理

AI 整理是可选功能。你启用 AI 并开始分析后，LinkSet 会将所选链接的网址、标题、描述、当前分组信息及已有分组名称发送给你选择的提供商：DeepSeek、OpenAI、Google Gemini 或你配置的兼容接口。AI 请求不包含截图。保存书签不会自动触发 AI 分析。接口连接测试发送示例请求，而非你的书签库。AI 返回整理建议，移动链接需要你确认。

AI 凭据与选择

你的 API Key 保存在当前设备的系统钥匙串中，用于向所选接口验证请求，不会作为书签记录同步到 iCloud。你可以关闭 AI 以停止新的整理请求，在 AI 设置中清除已保存的 Key，或向提供商撤销 Key。关闭 AI 无法撤回已经发送的数据。各提供商自行控制数据的处理、保留和删除；发送敏感信息前，请查看其政策，使用自定义接口时尤其如此。

购买服务

App Store 付款由 Apple 处理。LinkSet 使用 RevenueCat 加载商品、验证 Pro 权益和恢复购买，涉及购买及交易信息与匿名应用用户标识。LinkSet 不要求你注册独立账户，也不会向 RevenueCat 提供书签库或 AI Key。LinkSet 不会收到你的银行卡信息。Apple 与 RevenueCat 按各自政策处理其服务数据。

保留与删除

书签会保留到你主动删除为止。开启同步时，删除操作可以同步到其他设备。关闭同步本身不会清除之前已同步的 iCloud 数据。本机自动快照最多保留七份恢复文件；删除书签不会立即将其从已有快照或导出备份中移除。预览缓存可在设置中清理。导出文件需在其保存位置另行删除。钥匙串凭据可能在卸载应用后继续保留，请单独清除或撤销 API Key。卸载应用不一定删除 iCloud 数据、导出文件、购买记录或 AI 提供商已收到的数据。有关服务数据的删除方式，请联系相应服务；LinkSet 数据问题可联系我们协助。

设备权限与隐私分组

生物识别或设备密码验证通过 Apple 的系统认证服务完成，LinkSet 不会取得生物识别模板。你明确选择粘贴时，应用才读取剪贴板内容。隐藏和锁定分组控制 LinkSet 内的内容可见性，不会加密导出的备份，也不能替代设备安全。请保护设备及导出的文件。

联系与政策更新

LinkSet 不包含广告。如有隐私问题或需要数据删除协助，请联系 linksetapp@gmail.com。数据处理方式变化时，我们会更新政策，并标明新的生效日期。
